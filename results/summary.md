## Thermal calibration

Calibration configuration (uniform, N = 16384, 8 threads, static, 150 steps) was run 65 times by the thermal gate during the re-runs: min 0.97 s, median 1.15 s, max 2.57 s; reference (main schedule experiment) 1.21 s.  Runs only proceeded once the calibration was within 8 % of the reference.
